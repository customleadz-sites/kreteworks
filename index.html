09 — Good Questions

Before you call.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. Alex comes out, measures the site, talks through prep, thickness and finish, and gives you a written price before any work starts.

Are you insured?

Yes — Krete Works Concrete is fully insured.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and what's already on the schedule. Tell Alex when you need it done and he'll give you a straight answer with the quote, not a guess.

How is the price figured?

Size, thickness, how much site prep the ground needs, reinforcement, and the finish you want. All of it goes into one written number up front.

Do you do concrete repair, leveling or lifting?

No. We pour new concrete — slabs, driveways, patios, sidewalks, footers, foundations and walls.

Do you work with builders and contractors?

Yes. Footers, foundations, slabs and flatwork for builders are a big part of what we do. Call to talk through your plans and schedule.

What do I need to do before you show up?

Clear the area and let us know about anything underground. We handle the grading, forming, pouring, finishing and cleanup.
