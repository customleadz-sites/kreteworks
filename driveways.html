08 — Good Questions

Before you call.

How long before I can drive on a new driveway?

You can usually walk on it the next day. Alex will tell you exactly when it's ready for cars and when it's ready for trucks — it depends on the weather and the mix.

Can you extend or widen the driveway I already have?

Yes. We tie the new pour into the existing slab so it drains right and looks like it belongs.

Broom finish or smooth?

Broom finish is standard on driveways — it gives you grip when it's wet. If you want a smooth or different finish, just say so when Alex quotes it.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. Alex comes out, measures the site, talks through prep, thickness and finish, and gives you a written price before any work starts.

Are you insured?

Yes — Krete Works Concrete is fully insured.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and what's already on the schedule. Tell Alex when you need it done and he'll give you a straight answer with the quote, not a guess.

How is the price figured?

Size, thickness, how much site prep the ground needs, reinforcement, and the finish you want. All of it goes into one written number up front.
