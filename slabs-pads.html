08 — Good Questions

Before you call.

Do you pour the slab before or after the pole barn goes up?

Either. Some builders want the pad first, some set posts and have us pour inside them. Tell Alex which way your builder works and he'll plan the pour around it.

Can you work from my building supplier's drawings?

Yes. Send the slab and footer drawings that came with your metal building or pole barn kit and we'll pour to them.

How thick should a shop slab be?

It depends on what's going on it — a lawn mower and a lifted truck are different loads. Alex will recommend thickness and reinforcement when he quotes it, and it's all in the written price.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. Alex comes out, measures the site, talks through prep, thickness and finish, and gives you a written price before any work starts.

Are you insured?

Yes — Krete Works Concrete is fully insured.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and what's already on the schedule. Tell Alex when you need it done and he'll give you a straight answer with the quote, not a guess.

How is the price figured?

Size, thickness, how much site prep the ground needs, reinforcement, and the finish you want. All of it goes into one written number up front.
